Sophia

Sophia's full skill sheet: conquest skills Puppet Master, Scalding Mark and Scalding Mark - Lash, expedition skills Arcane Pact, Terror - Deathblow and Terror - Annihilation, and the Scarlet Rose widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Puppet Master

Sophia's subtle rose-magic saps enemy soldiers' resolve, dealing Attack * 140% area of effect damage and confusing them for 1s.

Scalding Mark

Sophia attacks with her enchanted rose, dealing Attack * 70% damage to the target and applying a Scalding Mark, increasing the target's damage taken by 6% for 4s.

Scalding Mark - Lash

Scalding Marks boost Sophia's Attack by 24% and allow her to lash afflicted targets, dealing 12% increased damage.

Expedition

Arcane Pact

The arcane pact grants Sophia the protection of night, and a 40% chance of reducing all squad damage received by 50% every turn.

Terror - Deathblow

Sophia radiates a fearsome aura that has a chance to inflict Terror on enemy targets. Enemy targets suffer the effects of Terror every 2 turns and will receive 200% increased Cavalry damage on the following turn. Terror lasts 1 turn.

Terror - Annihilation

Terror increases fragility. All Squads deal 75% increased damage to Terrified targets.
