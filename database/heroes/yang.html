Yang

Yang's full skill sheet: conquest skills Kin Throw, Combo and Deadshot, expedition skills Avalanche, Ice Zone and Ambush, and the Frostkin widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Kin Throw

Yang sends Frostkin in a straight line through the enemy, dealing Attack * 140% area of effect damage, then the same again on its return path.

Combo

Yang's special firearm has an 35% chance of inflicting a combo shot, dealing another round of damage to enemies.

Deadshot

Yang enjoys an 15% Crit Rate when dealing damage.

Expedition

Avalanche

An avalanche for Yang is just another weapon in his arsenal, resulting in an additional strike against a target by all Squads every 4 turns for 100% damage.

Ice Zone

Years of arctic adversity have taught Yang to seize advantages. Yang's Archers have a 40% chance of dealing 100% extra damage with every attack.

Ambush

Yang is an advanced thinker who can easily grasp the principles of almost any phenomena, giving all Squads a 40% chance of dealing increased damage every turn.
