Luna

Luna's full skill sheet: conquest skills Starfall, Arrow Storm and One Hunt, expedition skills Driving Light, Woodland Vengeance and Arrowflash, and the Envoy of Ancients widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Starfall

Luna channels the stars of heaven against a wicked enemy, dealing Attack * 300% damage to enemies within range and stunning them for 1.5s.

Arrow Storm

Luna looses a particularly brutal arrow against an enemy, dealing Attack * 140% damage to enemies within range.

One Hunt

Luna's career exterminating the disciples of evil has done wonders for her skills, increasing her Attack by 2.5% for each enemy defeated, up to 15 stacks.

Legendary · Gen. 8 · Archers

Skill level

Expedition

Driving Light

Luna's lunar light drives away wickedness, increasing total Squad's Attack by 25%.

Woodland Vengeance

Luna's thirst for vengeance is a snare of ill-fortune against enemies, reducing enemy Squad's Attack by 50% for 1 turn every 2 turns.

Arrowflash

Improvements in arrowheads have made their trajectory much less predictable, granting our archers an extra 50% damage to the target with each attack.
