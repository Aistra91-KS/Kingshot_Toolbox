Triton

Triton's full skill sheet: conquest skills Tidewill, Regal Wrath and Striking Hit, expedition skills Command of Power, Warfare of Power and Oath of Power, and the Tidal Scepter widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Tidewill

Triton surrounds himself with a barrier of waves that surges forward, gaining invulnerability for 2s before dealing Attack * 140% area of effect damage.

Regal Wrath

Triton channels the rage of the ruler within, increasing Attack by 24% and Defense by 48% for 4s.

Striking Hit

Triton lashes the disobedient, dealing Attack * 28% damage to a random enemy with every normal attack.

Expedition

Command of Power

Acceptance of Triton's authority enables the fruits to be shared thereof, increasing total Squads' Defense by 25%.

Warfare of Power

The power of warfare lies in shared fearlessness in the face of battle, increasing total Squads' skill damage dealt by 30%.

Oath of Power

Great power stems from a willingness to keep oaths, increasing Infantry Health by 20% as well as Cavalry and Archer Health by 30%.
