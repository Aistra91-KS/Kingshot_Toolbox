Conquest

Rejuvenating Plea

Hilde immediately restores Health equal to Attack * 280% to all friendly heroes.

Redeemer

Hilde immediately restores Health equal to Attack * 140% to the weakest friendly hero.

Intimidation

Hilde disables the enemy target for 1s while dealing Attack * 196% damage.
