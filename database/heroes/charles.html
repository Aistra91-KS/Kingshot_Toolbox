Charles

Charles's full skill sheet: conquest skills Vengeance, Parting Gift and Nimble Soul, expedition skills Intimidation, Iron Bodies and Great Justice, and the Justice Fist widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Vengeance

Charles unleashes a Vengeance strike from the Angel of Justice against enemies in a fan-shaped area ahead, dealing Attack * 140% damage, stunning targets for 1s and increasing his own Attack by 100% for 2s.

Parting Gift

Charles mines the area after his mech is destroyed, dealing Attack * 280% area of effect damage to nearby enemies.

Nimble Soul

Charles has always been gifted with lightning-fast reactions, granting an 50% chance of reducing damage taken by 50%.

Expedition

Intimidation

The terrifying presence of the massive Angel of Justice on the battlefield reduces enemy Squad's Total Lethality by 20%.

Iron Bodies

Superior metal armor forging blunts the impact of conventional weapons, reducing Squad's Damage Taken by 20%

Great Justice

The Angel of Justice comes outfitted with state-of-the-art battlefield medkits, increasing Squad's total Health by 25%.
