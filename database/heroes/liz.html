Conquest

Bubbletopia

Liz flings a great gaseous bubble toward the enemy, disabling the target for 1.5s and dealing Attack * 300% area of effect damage to it and nearby enemies.

Slow Medicine

Liz launches an extremely special slowing concoction, dealing Attack * 140% damage to an enemy and reducing the Attack Speed of surrounding enemies by 50% for 3s.

Quick Brew

Liz drinks her own nimble potion, increasing her Attack Speed by 30%.

Alloyed Agent

"My elixirs always work eventually. Just drink more!" This enhanced brew has a 50% chance of increasing total Squad's Lethality by 50% every turn.

Vitalis Potion

Liz's powerful revitalizing mixture increases total Squad's Health by 25%.

Horsemaster

Liz has cultivated an interest in horse feed, improvements to which gives Cavalry attacks a 50% chance of dealing 150% extra damage.
