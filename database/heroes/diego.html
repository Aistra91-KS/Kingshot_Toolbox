Diego

Diego's full skill sheet: conquest skills Undefendable, Blade Soul and Shadow Flicker, expedition skills Defensive Guidance, Piercing Aura and Cold Frost, and the Meteor Blade widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

Undefendable

Diego raises a shield absorbing damage equal to Attack * 380% for 5s.

Blade Soul

For 3s, Diego lowers enemy heroes' Attack by 5% and raises his own Attack by 5% for each hero affected.

Shadow Flicker

Diego gains an 15% chance of dodging normal attacks and an 20% Crit Rate.

Expedition

Defensive Guidance

A good offense is a good defense. Diego increases total Squad's Defense by 15% and total Health by 10%.

Piercing Aura

Diego's pride in his craft is easily shared, granting Squad's Attack a 50% chance of dealing 50% extra damage.

Cold Frost

Even rank-and-file enemies cower in the face of Diego's awesome reputation, increasing total Squad's Lethality by 25%.
