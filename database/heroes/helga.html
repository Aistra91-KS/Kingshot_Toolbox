Conquest

Antler Assault

Helga performs an antler charge, dealing Attack * 224% area of effect damage, knocking the targets back and stunning them for 1s.

Spear Charge

Helga deals Attack * 210% damage to the target.

Fury

When taking damage, Helga has a 10% chance of increasing Attack by 12% for 3s. Stacks up to 5 times.

Expedition

Oath of Guardian

Helga has a 40% chance of reducing damage taken by 50% for all squads.

Echoes of Valhalla

Helga blows the horn to motivate the squad, increasing the total Squads' Attack by 25%.

Nature's Balance

Helga invites everyone to embrace nature, increasing the total Squads' Lethality by 25%.
