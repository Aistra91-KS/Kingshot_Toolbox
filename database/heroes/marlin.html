Conquest

Tidal Rush

Marlin unleashes a tidal rush, dealing Attack * 280% area of effect damage and immobilizing the enemy for 1.5s.

Fermented Blow

Marlin delivers a fermented blow, dealing Attack * 70% area of effect damage.

Lucky Hits

Marlin lands lucky hits, stunning the target for 0.5s after every 5 strikes.
