Wee & Woo

Wee & Woo's full skill sheet: conquest skills High Ground, Incendiaries and Frontal Support, expedition skills Artillerymen, Chain Shelling and Boom Boom, and the Mortar widget. Every value, from level 1 to level 5.

Conquest

High Ground

Wee & Woo follow the artillery principle of high ground, long shots to the letter, dealing Attack * 420% area of effect damage.

Incendiaries

Wee & Woo fire a special incendiary round from the Royal Foundry, dealing Attack * 84% area of effect damage. The flaming crater in its wake also deals Attack * 25% damage to nearby enemies every 0.5s for 2s.

Frontal Support

Article 30 of the artilleryman's code provides a role for non-essential personnel at the front when necessary, increasing Attack by 26%.

Expedition

Artillerymen

The inclusion of the artillerymen has an overall helpful effect of increasing Squad's total Attack by 15% and total Lethality by 10%.

Chain Shelling

Wee & Woo's devastating back-to-back rounds tear shreds through the enemy lines, increasing Squad's Damage Dealt to Archer by 30% and to Infantry by 25%.

Boom Boom

There's always someone in every cannon duo who can aim well. Wee & Woo's presence gives Squad's Attack a 50% chance of dealing 50% extra damage.
