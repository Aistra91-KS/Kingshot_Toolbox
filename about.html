About

I'm Aistra, a Kingshot player on server 286.

This site doesn't claim to be the reference on the game. It is first and foremost the result of a personal interest in vibecoding — an approach to development I'm passionate about — which I naturally wanted to tie to a game I enjoy.

The tools you'll find here are built to pragmatically answer my own in-game needs, as well as those of my alliance members. Designed in the first place to save us time and get more out of the game, they are made available in the hope that they can be useful to other players too.

Have fun.

— Aistra

Good to know

  • Your data stays with you. Everything you enter — hero levels, stocks, formations, carts — is saved in your browser only. There is no account, no server, and nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it; the “Global Backup” button exports it as a file so you can move it to another device.
  • Several accounts, several profiles. Main account, farm account… each profile keeps its own data, and you switch between them from the badge in the top bar.
  • Game data is entered by hand. Costs, levels and effects come from in-game readings and from the community. Mistakes happen, and the game changes — if a number looks wrong to you, it probably is worth reporting.
  • Free, bilingual, no ads. The whole site works in French and English, switches language without reloading, and has no ads or trackers.
