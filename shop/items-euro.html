Real-money item values

What each item really costs, worked out from the paid pack it appears in: the pack's price divided by how many you get. Only packs you can buy at least once a month are counted: event packs, VIP packs and one-off offers are left out - a price you cannot get again next month is not a reference price. Three rules correct what that sum alone gets wrong - speedups follow a per-minute scale, a few items are worked out from another one, and raw resources are weighted down. A pill marks them in the table and every rule is spelled out below. This table is read-only and independent from the gem values - no exchange rate is ever worked out between the two.
