Real-money item values

What each item really costs, worked out from the paid pack it appears in: the pack's price divided by how many you get. A few items no pack sells are worked out from an item that a pack does price - they are marked “Calculated”, and the maths is spelled out under the table. This table is read-only and independent from the gem values - no exchange rate is ever worked out between the two.
