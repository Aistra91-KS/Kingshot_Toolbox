Reading the return

Three events are broken down day by day: Adventure Stall, Dragon's Caravan and Theater Shop. Their page adds a section under the item table where you tick the packs you bought, and it totals what came back.

The percentage is the value received divided by the money spent, both in the same currency. Nothing converts gems into euros: the gem value sits beside the percentage, never inside it. Spend nothing and there is no percentage to show - the tile says F2P.

  • < 100 %Bad dealyou got back less than you paid
  • 100 - 120 %Fairroughly the price of the same items bought elsewhere
  • 120 - 140 %Gooda real gain, without being exceptional
  • 140 - 180 %Very goodamong the better events of a season
  • 180 %+Excellentthe event pays for itself nearly twice over

One caveat worth knowing: an item is only worth what you would have bought anyway. A pack full of speedups you will never burn scores well and still is not a good purchase.
